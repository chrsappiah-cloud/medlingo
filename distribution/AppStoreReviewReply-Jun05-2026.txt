Guideline 2.1(a) - Account state and Sign Out

We found two issues:

1. The Account screen was hardcoded to display a named avatar initial ("C") regardless of authentication state. On a fresh or clean install, this made the account appear to belong to a specific named user. This has been corrected — the avatar now shows a generic person icon when no account is signed in.

2. The display name and email fields were showing a hardcoded user profile. These now correctly show "Guest learner" and "No account signed in" on a clean launch.

Additional corrections:

- Stale local auth tokens from older builds are cleared during launch.
- The Sign Out button is only shown when a real user session exists.
- When Sign Out is available, tapping it clears the session immediately and returns Account to the guest state.
